Key Features & Benefits:

Alternator Mastery: Dual PWM drivers for precise field control (up to 20A), with real-time current/voltage monitoring via INA226. Prevent overcharge and extend battery life – perfect for LiFePO4 setups!
Tank Level Monitoring: Supports two pressure sensors (0-1m range) for accurate fuel/water tank readings. No more guesswork – get alerts via WiFi/Bluetooth.
Thermostat & Relay Control: Two DS18B20 temp sensors and relays (10A/240V) for generic automation, like fridge compressors or engine cooling. Set it and forget it!
Battery & RPM Insights: Continuous 12V battery monitoring plus RPM input for engine health checks. Stay ahead of issues with CAN bus support for Victron/Raymarine/Yanmar.
Marine-Ready Design: 9-32V input, EMI filtering, opto-isolation, and screw terminals for easy wiring. Compact size (ESP32-S3-WROOM-2 module) with on-board antenna – fits in tight engine rooms.
Smart Integration: WiFi/Bluetooth for remote access, error buzzer/LED for diagnostics, and deep sleep mode for low power (under 500mA). Seamless with Home Assistant or SignalK.
